# EduStream — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Mixed audience: high school students, university students, and working professionals, all with different levels of digital literacy. Usage time is unpredictable — could be a student cramming at midnight or a professional browsing during a lunch break. The platform must feel instantly legible to a first-timer but have the kind of quiet depth that rewards regular users. Egyptian / MENA primary market (Vodafone Cash as payment signal).

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: Decisive. Scholarly. Authoritative.

The brand voice is high-register academic and intentional — "Digital Atheneum", "sanctuary for the mind." This is a deliberate positioning choice, not a placeholder. The platform should feel like a Bloomberg Terminal crossed with a premium academic press catalogue: efficient, credible, and precise — not cluttered, not corporate, not generic SaaS.

Emotional goals: **confidence** (the student feels they're accessing something of genuine quality), **momentum** (the interface gets out of the way and lets them learn), **distinction** (the platform feels unlike anything they've used before).

### Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone**: Editorial authority. Dark and light modes both supported, each intentionally designed — not simply inverted from one another. Indigo primary is locked in and must be preserved.

**Typography**:
- Display / headings: **Bricolage Grotesque** — a humanist grotesque with subtle editorial character. Not a typical tech sans, not a fussy serif. Downloads from Google Fonts.
- Body / UI: **Geist** — already installed as `@fontsource-variable/geist`. Technical, clean, just slightly cool. Perfect for a "fast, professional" brand that also carries scholarly weight.

**Color approach**:
- Primary: `oklch(0.35 0.18 268)` — indigo. Preserved exactly.
- Tint all neutrals toward the indigo hue (already established, continue this).
- Light mode: Near-white surfaces, tinted slightly toward indigo. Indigo ink feels authoritative on pale ground.
- Dark mode: Deep near-black surfaces `oklch(0.12 0.015 268)`, with elevated surfaces at `oklch(0.18 0.015 268)`. NOT the typical cyan-on-dark AI palette. Indigo accents on dark feel rare and considered.
- Accent: Emerald `oklch(0.55 0.20 160)` — kept as a precise signal color for success/progress states, used sparingly.
- NEVER: cyan glows, purple-to-blue gradients, neon on dark.

**Motion**: Fast and purposeful. Ease-out-quart decelerations, staggered entry reveals on page load. No bounce, no elastic. Motion conveys structure, not decoration.

**Anti-references** (what EduStream must NOT look like):
- Udemy: Overstuffed marketplace grid, orange CTAs everywhere, every piece of content competing for attention
- Coursera: Corporate blue, stiff layout, feels like an enterprise HR portal
- Generic SaaS landing pages: Hero number stats, "Join 10,000 users" social proof bars, identical card grids with icon + heading + text
- The typical "AI-designed" aesthetic: glassmorphism everywhere, gradient text, glowing purple/cyan accents on dark

### Design Principles

1. **Editorial hierarchy first** — Every screen has a single dominant visual message. Supporting information is visible but subordinate. No page competes with itself.

2. **Indigo as signal, not decoration** — The primary color appears where it must be noticed: active states, primary CTAs, key data points. It loses meaning if it's everywhere.

3. **Dual-mode with intention** — Dark mode is not just an inverted light mode. Dark mode is for focused, immersive learning sessions. Light mode is for browsing, discovery, and administrative tasks. Each mode has its own spatial logic.

4. **Speed as respect** — The interface gets out of the way. Short copy, clear labels, decisive actions. Students and professionals don't have time to decipher. Every word earns its place.

5. **Density calibrated to context** — The landing page is spacious and editorial. The student dashboard is compact and data-forward. The teacher management view is functional and precise. Don't apply the same spacing rhythm everywhere.

## Technical Constraints

- **Framework**: React 19 + Vite + TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS 4.0 + DaisyUI 5.0
- **Fonts installed**: `@fontsource-variable/geist` (body)
- **Motion**: `motion/react` (Framer Motion v11+)
- **Icons**: `lucide-react` + `react-icons/hi2`
- **Theming**: DaisyUI plugin with OKLCH-based semantic tokens; custom `@theme` block in `index.css`
- **Accessibility**: No specific WCAG target stated; apply sensible defaults (focus rings, contrast ratios ≥ 4.5:1 for body text)
